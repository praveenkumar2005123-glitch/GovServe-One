# Initial Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized access | Medium | High | RBAC, MFA, server-side authorization |
| Data breach | Medium | Critical | Encryption, minimization, monitoring, incident response |
| Service outage | Medium | High | Redundancy, backups, recovery drills |
| Scope creep | High | Medium | Change control and prioritized backlog |
| Low digital literacy | Medium | Medium | Simple UX, multilingual support, assisted channels |
| Integration delays | Medium | High | Mock APIs, interface contracts, phased integration |
